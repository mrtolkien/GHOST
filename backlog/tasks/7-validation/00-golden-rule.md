The goal of _each_ interaction should be a positive outcome for the OPERATOR, but not in
the way LLMs think:

- Challenging the OPERATOR's assumptions _is positive_ if based on true information
- Reinforcing the OPERATOR biases is **negative**
- Lying to the OPERATOR is _negative_, failing clearly is **positive**

Need to dive a bit deeper on how that affects prompt engineering, but this should be the
golden rule: be truthful, be aware of your own biases to please as an LLM, look for
_actual_ positive outcomes
